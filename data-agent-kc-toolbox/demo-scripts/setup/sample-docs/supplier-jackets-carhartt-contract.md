# Supplier Agreement — Carhartt Workwear & Outerwear

**Vendor**: Carhartt, Inc.
**Cymbal Retail SKU prefix**: CAR-OUTW-*
**Product category covered**: Outerwear & Coats (Jackets, Coats, Parkas, Vests, Outerwear Accessories)
**Effective**: 2024-01-15 — 2026-12-31
**Cymbal contract owner**: Procurement Team / cymbal-procurement@cymbal-retail.example
**Carhartt account manager**: jorge.menendez@carhartt.example

## 1. Scope of Supply

Carhartt agrees to manufacture and supply Outerwear & Coats products to Cymbal Retail's distribution centers on a purchase-order basis. Cymbal Retail commits to a minimum annual order volume of 1,500 line items across all SKUs in the Outerwear & Coats category.

## 2. Pricing and Margin

- Wholesale unit pricing is per Schedule A (not reproduced here; current effective sheet on file with Procurement).
- Cymbal Retail's **target wholesale-to-retail markup for Carhartt outerwear is 1.85× – 1.95×**. Realized markup is reviewed quarterly:
  - **Above 1.95×** → healthy, no action.
  - **1.85× – 1.95×** → on target.
  - **Below 1.85×** → margin compression review with Procurement and Merchandising.
- Volume rebates of 2% (annual) apply after the 1,500 line-item threshold is exceeded.

> **Operational note**: Realized markup is defined as `SUM(order_items.sale_price) / SUM(products.cost)` over non-cancelled, non-returned line items for SKUs matching the CAR-OUTW-* prefix in a given period. The Data Team's quarterly margin report uses this definition.

## 3. Quality and Returns

### 3.1 Defect rate

Defect rate must remain below **0.8%** of shipped units. Defective items returned to Carhartt are credited at full wholesale.

### 3.2 Customer return rate

Customer return rate (returns initiated by end customers for non-defect reasons) is informational from Carhartt's perspective — it does not flow back as a credit. **However**, Carhartt's outerwear is held accountable to Cymbal Retail's customer-facing return-rate alert threshold (currently 8%, per `return-policy-2024.md`, Section 4). Brands consistently above this threshold for two consecutive months trigger a joint vendor business review focused on:

- Product quality and sizing accuracy
- Photography and listing description accuracy
- Promotional positioning that may set wrong customer expectations

## 4. Shipping

Carhartt commits to ship purchase orders from its distribution warehouses within **5 business days** of PO acknowledgement. This is a target window; shipping reliability has been excellent (no breach incidents in the prior 24 months), so the agreement does not currently include a per-shipment penalty structure. Cymbal Retail reserves the right to re-introduce per-incident penalties at the next renewal if performance degrades.

## 5. Reporting and Reviews

- Carhartt provides weekly shipping reports to Cymbal Retail Procurement.
- Cymbal Retail's Data Team produces a monthly Carhartt-program scorecard covering: realized markup (Section 2), customer return rate (Section 3.2), volume vs the annual commitment (Section 1), and ship-time distribution (Section 4).
- Quarterly business review with Carhartt's account manager covers any scorecard signals outside target.
