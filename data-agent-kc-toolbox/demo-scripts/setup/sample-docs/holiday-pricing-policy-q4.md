# Q4 Holiday Pricing & Promotions Policy

**Effective**: 2024-10-15 — 2024-12-31
**Owner**: Merchandising Team / cymbal-merch@cymbal-retail.example

## 1. Promotion windows

| Window | Dates | Discount cap | Categories included |
|---|---|---|---|
| Pre-Holiday Tease | Oct 15 – Oct 31 | 15% | All except Outerwear |
| Black Friday | Nov 28 – Dec 1 | 35% | All categories |
| Cyber Week | Dec 2 – Dec 8 | 30% | All categories |
| Mid-December | Dec 9 – Dec 18 | 25% | Non-clearance only |
| Last-Minute / Gift Card Window | Dec 19 – Dec 24 | 20% | All categories |
| Post-Holiday Clearance | Dec 26 – Dec 31 | 50% | Clearance flag only |

## 2. Outerwear-specific rules

Outerwear is **excluded from the Pre-Holiday Tease window** (Oct 15 – Oct 31) and is **capped at 25% off through Black Friday** to preserve margin during peak demand. This is a deliberate strategy to protect the 1.85× – 1.95× wholesale-to-retail markup target for Apollo Athletic Goods supplied SKUs (see supplier agreement).

If a Q4 outerwear-revenue review surfaces margin compression, it is **not** expected to be driven by discounting policy — the discount caps prevent that. Investigate volume (orders), shipping delays, or product mix instead.

## 3. Free shipping thresholds

- Standard Cymbal Retail free shipping: $75 minimum order value.
- During Q4 the threshold drops to **$50** for orders placed Nov 28 – Dec 24.
- Members of the Cymbal+ loyalty program ship free year-round and are unaffected.
